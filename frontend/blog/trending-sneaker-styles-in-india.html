Sneaker Styles Worth Knowing in India

By Kicks Aura Team · 9 November 2026

Sneaker fashion moves quickly, but most pairs fall into a few lasting styles. Knowing what sets them apart makes it much easier to pick something that suits you, rather than simply buying whatever is popular this month.

Clean low-tops

Simple shapes with minimal detailing, usually in white or neutral colours. They are the most versatile style and can move from jeans to chinos to shorts. If you want just one pair, start here.

Retro runners

Inspired by older running shoes, these have layered panels, mixed materials and a slightly sculpted sole. They add interest to plain outfits, and their mesh panels tend to feel comfortable in warm weather.

Chunky soles

Thick, exaggerated soles give a bolder look and a little extra height. They balance relaxed or wide-leg trousers well. Do check the weight before you buy, as some chunky pairs can feel heavy over a long day.

Slim, low-profile pairs

Thin gum soles and a narrow shape, often in suede or leather, inspired by classic indoor sports shoes. They look neat with straight or slightly cropped trousers, and they are a good alternative to a bulkier sneaker.

High-tops

High-tops cover the ankle and make a stronger statement. They are best in cooler months in much of India, since they can feel warm in peak summer. Wear them with slim or cropped trousers so the collar stays visible.

What suits Indian weather?

In hot and humid months, breathable mesh and lighter pairs are usually the most comfortable. During the monsoon, keep suede and light canvas at home and choose materials that wipe clean. Winter is the best time for leather, suede and high-tops.

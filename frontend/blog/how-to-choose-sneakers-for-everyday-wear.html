How to Choose the Perfect Sneakers for Everyday Wear

By Kicks Aura Team · 9 November 2026

The best everyday sneaker is rarely the most eye-catching pair. It is the one you reach for without thinking because it is comfortable, goes with most of your clothes and copes with the way you actually spend your day. These are the things worth checking before you buy.

Start with fit, not looks

Your toes should have about a thumb's width of room at the front, and your heel should stay in place when you walk. Feet swell slightly through the day, so a pair that feels tight at the start of the day rarely feels better by evening. Check the size chart on the product page and compare it with a pair you already wear comfortably.

Check the sole

For long days on your feet, look for a sole with some cushioning and enough thickness to soften hard pavements. Very flat, thin soles look neat but can get tiring after a few hours. If you walk on wet roads during the monsoon, a textured outsole will grip better than a smooth one.

Choose a material for your routine

  • Leather or leather-like uppers look smart, wipe clean easily and suit office-casual outfits.
  • Mesh and knit uppers are lighter and breathe better, which helps in hot weather.
  • Canvas is easy and relaxed, though it marks more easily and takes longer to dry.
  • Suede looks great but needs more care, so it is best kept away from rain.

Pick colours that work hard

If this will be your main pair, white, off-white, grey and black go with almost everything. Save bright colours and bold patterns for a second pair. A simple test: think of three outfits you wear most often. If the sneaker works with all three, it is a good everyday choice.

Think about upkeep

White pairs show dirt quickly on dusty roads, while darker or mixed-colour pairs hide it better. Whichever you choose, a quick wipe after wearing makes a big difference. Our sneaker care guide covers the details.

A Simple Guide to Casual Footwear for Men

By Kicks Aura Team · 9 November 2026

You do not need a shelf full of shoes to be covered for most occasions. A small, considered rotation is easier to look after and much simpler to dress around. Here is how to think about it.

The everyday sneaker

This is the pair you wear most, so prioritise comfort and a neutral colour. A clean low-top works for college, casual workplaces, errands and weekends. Our guide to choosing everyday sneakers explains what to look for.

The statement sneaker

A second pair with more colour or a bolder shape gives your outfits some variety. Wear it with plain clothes so the shoes can stand out, and rotate it with your everyday pair so that both last longer.

Loafers or smart casual shoes

For dinners, family events and smarter workplaces, a simple loafer or leather shoe bridges the gap between sneakers and formal shoes. It works with chinos and well-fitted trousers.

Sliders and flip-flops

They are not for every outing, but they are ideal for quick trips, travel and rainy days when you would rather not risk your other shoes. Choose a pair with a textured sole for better grip on wet floors.

Making your shoes last

  • Avoid wearing the same pair every day, so they can air out between wears.
  • Dry wet shoes away from direct heat, which can damage glue and materials.
  • Use a shoe bag or box when storing shoes for long periods, especially in humid months.

Finish the look

Small accessories pull an outfit together. A belt matched to your shoes and a simple watch can make casual clothes look far more considered.

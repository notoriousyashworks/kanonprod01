How to Style Sneakers with Different Outfits

By Kicks Aura Team · 9 November 2026

Sneakers go with far more than jeans. The trick is to match the shape and colour of the shoe to the mood of the outfit. Here are simple combinations that work without much effort.

Jeans and a T-shirt

The classic for a reason. White low-tops look sharp with dark denim, while retro runners add character to lighter washes. If your jeans are long, a small cuff shows off the shoe and tidies the line of the leg.

Chinos and a shirt

For office-casual days, choose clean leather or leather-like sneakers in white, grey or black with minimal branding and colour. Keep the rest of the outfit crisp: an ironed shirt and well-fitted chinos stop the look from feeling too casual.

Shorts

Lighter, simpler sneakers work best because they do not feel heavy against bare legs. Low or no-show socks keep the look clean. Very chunky pairs can overpower shorts unless that is the look you want.

Dresses and skirts

White sneakers with a summer dress are comfortable and easy for a day out. For a more modern look, try a chunky sole with a midi skirt; the contrast between the two is what makes it work.

Kurtas and Indo-western outfits

A plain kurta with clean white or neutral sneakers is a relaxed choice for casual gatherings and day events. Stick to simple shoes so the kurta stays the focus, and save formal footwear for formal occasions.

Three rules that always help

  • Pick up one colour from your outfit in your sneakers, or keep the shoes neutral.
  • Pay attention to trouser length: a clean break above the shoe looks tidier than fabric bunching over it.
  • Keep them clean. Fresh sneakers make even a simple outfit look put together.
